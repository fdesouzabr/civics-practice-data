Civics Practice

Study app for the 2025 U.S. naturalization civics test.

Practice all 128 questions by typing or speaking your answers, take mock interviews that stop at 12 correct or 9 missed, and track what you have learned. Everything runs on your iPhone.

Help

  • Questions about your state (senators, representative, governor, capital) appear after you add your state and officials in Settings.
  • A more natural reading voice: in the iPhone Settings app, go to Accessibility > Read & Speak (or Spoken Content) > Voices > English and download a Premium or Enhanced voice.
  • Answers that change with elections (President, Vice President, Speaker, Chief Justice) update automatically. Always check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview.
