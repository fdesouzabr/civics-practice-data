Privacy Policy

Civics Practice for iPhone. Last updated October 3, 2026.

Civics Practice does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts and no tracking. The app has no sign-in, analytics, or advertising.
  • Your progress and settings (scores, your state, and the names you type) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Microphone and speech recognition are used only when you tap the microphone to answer a question. Speech is converted to text on your device when your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy. The app does not record or keep audio.
  • Smart grading uses Apple Intelligence on your device. Your answers are not sent to the developer.
  • Network use is limited to downloading a public list of current federal officials from this website's repository. No information about you is sent.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.

Civics Practice is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services or any government agency.
